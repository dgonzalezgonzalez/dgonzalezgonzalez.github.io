OECD consultancy

Food systems policy analysis, digital tools, and AI capacity building

Consulting work for the OECD Trade and Agriculture Directorate's Agro-food Trade and Markets division on demand-side policies, food loss and waste, and practical tools for food systems policy. The work combines policy research, empirical analysis, applied AI, and digital development to make evidence more usable for policy-makers and OECD teams.

My contribution has included policy stocktaking, literature review, classification of policy instruments, and comparative analysis of approaches such as consumer education, voluntary collaboration, regulation, fiscal measures, public procurement, food-based dietary guidelines, and public canteens. The work also examines stakeholder engagement, household acceptability, evidence gaps, and trade-offs with affordability and public health.

On the technical side, I developed retrieval-augmented generation workflows and semantic analysis tools to structure policy information, compute cosine-similarity policy-alignment measures, extract evidence on policy effectiveness and evaluation results, and compare policy texts across strategic dimensions. I also used text embeddings and k-means clustering for semantic policy classification, and carried out econometric analysis to estimate the effect of Denmark's livestock emissions tax announcement on beef consumer prices, using difference-in-differences, synthetic control, and synthetic difference-in-differences methods.

In my current consultancy, I am extending this work through a RAG-based meta-evaluation of food loss and waste policies, classifying evaluated policies and synthesising evidence on their effectiveness. I am also developing an interactive online resource for the OECD Toolkit for Equal Opportunities in Food Systems, including a policy-facing dashboard, self-assessment features, and a specialised RAG-based chatbot built on the relevant documentation.

A third part of the assignment focuses on capacity building: preparing materials and teaching a practical course on AI applications to policy analysis for the OECD Food Systems Team. The course covers embeddings, retrieval-augmented generation, agentic coding workflows, MCP, skills, Git basics, and hands-on examples using policy data.

Fun GitHub projects

  • faolex-analysis - analysis of more than 40,000 FAOLEX food legislation records, including demand-side and supply-side classification, abstract-based embeddings, translation caching, cosine-similarity analysis against food-policy strategy queries, time trends, country maps, and a unified interactive dashboard.
  • canarias-uni-ml - Python pipeline for Canary Islands job postings and Spanish university degree catalogs. It scrapes job sources, normalizes geography and contract fields, builds a degree catalog, gates candidate job-degree pairs, computes text-embedding alignment scores, and stores outputs in CSV and SQLite.
  • prestaciones-por-empleo - Python pipeline for SEPE monthly unemployment-benefit reports. It detects and downloads new Excel files, processes selected benefit sheets, creates a wide Spanish-language dataset, generates AIReF-style static figures, and builds a local HTML dashboard with filters by sex, age, region, province, and period.
  • Prestaciones-por-empleo-AI-exposure - EPA-focused Spanish labor-market AI exposure pipeline based on Anthropic occupation exposure data, O*NET descriptions, INE CNO-2011 occupation records, and EPA microdata. It builds taxonomy-aware CNO4 occupation embeddings with local Ollama, predicts exposure using Random Forest, cosine-weighted, and cosine-nearest methods, aggregates CNO4 predictions to OCUP1 and then to industry-quarter exposure, and stores diagnostics and outputs in SQLite and CSV files.
  • replication-tracker-jpe-ej-ectj - local-first tracker for JPE, JPE Macroeconomics, JPE Microeconomics, The Economic Journal, and The Econometrics Journal. It pulls paper metadata from OpenAlex, maps papers to Dataverse and Zenodo replication repositories, reads repository files and documentation, classifies data availability, and exports dashboard JSON.
  • codex-claude-setup - portable backup of local Codex and Claude configuration, including rules, skills, agents, hooks, and plugins, while excluding caches, logs, session histories, and temporary databases.
